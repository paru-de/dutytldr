# The Gilded Araya (Asura)

#trial #ew

### Tank

**TB**: Cutting Jewel

### Healer

**Raidwide**: Lower Realm, Ephemerality

### Notable mechanics
- Ephemerality: Clones will cleave circle around them.
- Iconography: Big Clone repeats boss casts.
  - Pedestal Purge: Out.
  - Wheel of Deincarnation: Donut.
  - Bladewise: Line AoE. Clone Line AoE is HUGE, go to sides.
  - **Clone get's tp'd to random corner the next time Iconography is cast**.
- Six-bladed Khadga: Six half-cleaves in order of appearance.
  - Traveling clockwise or counterclockwise.
  - Last three are a repeat of the first three.
- The Face of Wrath / Delight: Half matching the flashed mask's color explodes.

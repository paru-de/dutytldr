## 1/9 [Mateus, the Corrupt]

### Tank
**TB**: Flash Freeze (untelegraphed, cleave)

### Notable mechanics
- Unbind: A dancer moves along the spiral
  - Aqua Sphere: Kill before dancer touches it
  - Gather two of the blue orbs, stand off the spiral
  - Water spouts: Three players on each. Blizzard III pillars forms if not done correctly
  - Any Blizzard III need to be destroyed asap
- Suffocation: Breathless Debuff
  - Kill Flume Toads, they drop air pockets
  - Do not let Breathless Debuff hit 10 stacks. Use air pockets to drop stacks.
  - Targeted Line AoE: Point away from air pockets

## 6/9 [Rofocale]

### Tank
**TB**: [Multi] Crush Helm (stacks, give Phys Vuln Up, can be Esuna'd)

### Healer
**Esuna**: Crush Helm (stacks 4 times)

### Notable mechanics
- Chariot: Line AoE. If marked, run close to the boss.
- Cry of Victory: Large cleave, directly after Chariot and Trample. Pre-move near boss.
- Trample: Figure 8. Upper/Lower part of 8 and outside is safe. 
- Three clones: Create Line AoE. Move to first clone that spawns in for safe zone.
- Three Adds: Alliance split. Stand towards centre of arena.
- Heavenly Subjugation: Ultimate, less damage when standing on the side.
- Embrace: Blue Circle AoE, invisible after a few seconds.
- Dark  Geas: Interact with, don't stand in circles. Immediately followed by clones with Line AoE.

## 9/9 [Argath Thadalfus]

*Getting hit with avoidable mechanics results in a stacking debuff. 3 stacks = loss of control for 7s.*

### Tank
**TB**: [L] Crippling Blow

### Healer
**Raidwide**: Fire IV

### Notable mechanics

**Commands**
Angel Face: Obey commands.
Demon Face: Disobey commands.
 - Triangle/PLay button: Move
 - Eye: Look away 

**Main mechanics**
- Blue Swords: Plus-shaped AoE, stand directly in center
- Adds: Kill, cluster them. They turn into shards which need to be burned down.
- Gnawing Dread: Forced movement in pointer direction. Includes a donut.

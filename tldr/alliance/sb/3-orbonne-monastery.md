## 3/6 [Agrias]

### Tank

**TB**: Thunder Slash (cleaving conal)

### Healer

**Raidwide**: Divine Light

### Notable Mechanics
*Duty actions: Shield gives dmg immunity **from the front**. Any action incl. moving will cancel it. Sword gives 180° attack effective against shielded enemies*.

- Judgment Blade: Use SHIELD Duty Action, while facing Agrias
- Alliance Split:
  - Small arena: Grab sword. Duty action for white adds, normal dps for black adds.
  - Main arena: Grab sword. Use Duty action to free trapped players.
- Hallowed Bolt: Stack markers. Either Donut followed by Out or Out followed by Donut.
- Adds: Use Duty Action (Sword) on shields tethered to add. Use Duty Action (Shield) to block large AoE if needed.
- Divine Ruination: Tether turns into Line AoE. Use Shield if tethered.

## 5/6 [The Thunder God]

### Tank

**TB**: Crush Helm, 5 hits with 4 stacks of Phys Vuln Up

**Crush Armor**: Do not take the tether! DPS mechanic.

### Healer
*All tanks receive auto-attacks.*
**Raidwide**: Balance Asunder (Ultimate, after adds)
**Doom**: [99%] Cleansing Strike, Heal to full.
**Esuna**: Crush Helm, 4 stacks of Phys Vuln Up.

### Notable Mechanics

**T.G. Holy Sword**
- Swords in ground: Donut
- Swords sideways: Out
- Swords above platform: Large circular AoE on platform below swords. Can target multiple platforms in sequence.

**Main mechanics**
- Shadowblade: Expanding AoE. Drop on back of nearby platform, DO NOT STACK.
- Duskblade: Towers, needs 3 players each.
- Crush Weapon: Go left.
- Crush Armor: Take tether from tank. Rotate players after each hit.

## 6/6 [Ultima, the High Seraph]

### Tank
*Off-tank: Demi-Virgo (3rd cast): Grab tether*

**TB**: [L] Redemption


### Healer

**Raidwide**: Dome after Demi's deals constant damage.

### Notable Mechanics
- Auralight: Out and Line AoE targeted at 3 players. Do not touch ice walls.
- Grand Cross: Plus-shaped Line AoE's. First cast has safespot directly on left or right of boss hitbox.

**Summon Demi**
- Demi-Aquarius: Traveling water jets. Paired with Auralight.
- Demi-Aries: Stand on slowest clock, then move into exploded square. Paired with AoE circles under some players.
- Demi-Leo: Two falling pillars. Paired with AoE circles under some players.
- All three: Clocks, Dorito's/Kiss, proximity marker (floating hammer), followed by half-wide cleave (check walls of arena).

**Phase 2 & 3**
*AoE's will be moved by one space into direction of the wind.*

- Demi-Virgo (2nd cast): Moving arrows. Each must have a player standing in empty circle at the end of path.
- Demi-Virgo (3rd cast): Untargetable ad. Drops blue AoE traps which turn invisible.

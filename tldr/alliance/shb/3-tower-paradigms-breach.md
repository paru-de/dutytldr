## 1/6 [Knave of Hearts]

### Tank
- **TB**: Magic Artillery Beta (each tank, splash)

### Healer
- **Raidwide**: Roar

### Notable mechanics
- Colossal Impact
  - Glowing red mouth: Sides are safe
  - Boss moves back: Center is safe
- Replicate: Cubes show order. Clones also cast Colossal Impact
- Spheroids: When paired with Magic Artillery Alpha: Alternating Line AoE's.
- Boxes: Use to avoid knockbacks. Mind simultanous Colossal Impact.

## 2/6 [Hansel & Gretel]
Shielded boss: Reflects damage unless attacked from front

### Tank
Separate both bosses

### Healer
- **Raidwide**: Wail, [L] Lamentation (when one boss is down)

### Notable mechanics
*Shielded boss: Reflects damage unless attacked from front. TB and Raidwide swaps between bosses*. 
- Bloody Sweep: Cleave from glowing lances
  - Lances are outside: Between bosses is safe
  - Lances are inside: Opposite of arena is safe
  - Arrows between them: Safe zones swap

- Breakthrough: Cleave, stand on edge opposite of lances
- Hungry Lance: Conal AoE, edge side of boss is safe

## 4/6 [Red Girl]

### Tank
- **TB**: Manipulate Energy (each tank, splash)

### Healer
- **Raidwide**: [L] Cruelty 

### Notable mechanics

*Color mechanic*
- Shockwave: Position circle next to blocks with *opposite* color
 - Same mechanic is used to free alliance trapped in center
- Hide from line AoE by standing behind block with same color (white laser = hide behind white block)

*Main mechanics*
- Replicate: 120° traveling Conal in direction of small Red Girls. 
  - Stand next to her, move into second Conal, then wait and move into second Conal after that
- Minigame: Use F-0 Switch to change laser and drone color. Opposite color destroys, same color protects from AoE. Focus adds.
- Child's Play: Forced march into indicated direction. *Do not use Sprint*.
- Replicate (P2): Small Red Girls will be moved into indicated direction. One spot won't have a Red Girl.

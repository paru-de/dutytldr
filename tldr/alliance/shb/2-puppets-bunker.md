## 1/6 [813P-operated Aegis Unit]

### Tank
**TB**: Firing Order: Anti-personnel Laser

### Healer
**Raidwide**: Maneuver: Diffusion Cannon

### Notable mechanics
- Maneuver: Beam Cannons: Overlapping AoE slowly focuses to create a safe-zone. Look for darker, shrinking areas.
- Maneuver: Refraction Cannons: Untelegraphed Cone AoE. Stand opposite of cannon.
- Program: Life's Last Song: Find opening in golden rings.

## 2/6 Trash pack
Interrupt **Maneuver: Volt Array**. Applies raidwide Paralysis. 

## 3/6 [Superior Flight Units]

### Tank
**TB**: Maneuver: High-powered Laser (Line)

### Healer
**Raidwide**: Maneuver: Volt Array

### Notable mechanics
- Maneuver: High-powered Laser: Stack marker, **do not aim at other parties**
- Formation: Sharp Turn: Watch sword
  - Outside in direction of travel: Center safe
  - Inside in direction of travel: Stand outside, watch Line AoE from other units
- Formation: Air Raid: Exaflare, stand next to Exaflare, move into path once clear
- Formation: Sliding Swipe: Alternating Half-wide cleave. Watch swords for order.

## 4/6 [905P-operated Heavy Artillery Unit]

### Tank
**TB**: Maneuver: Precision Guided Missile

### Healer
**Raidwide**: Maneuver: Missile Command (one hit for each Flight Unit still alive)

### Notable mechanics
- Operation: Activate Laser Turret: Stand on outside edge of top laser cannons, move into bottom laser cannon once safe
- Maneuver: Impact Crusher: Third jump location becomes donut
- Operation: Pod Program
  - R010: Line AoE, stand in-between two R010 pods
  - R030: Circle AoE centered on pods
  - All numbers the same - stand in-between pods
  - Different numbers: Look for two R010
  - **Mind the tethers shifting which pods executes R010/030**

## 6/6 [Compound 2P]

### Tank
**TB**: Compound Pod: R012 (Magic Vuln)

### Healer
**Raidwide**: Centrifugal Slice

### Notable mechanics
- Prime Blade:
  - Sword in ground: Donut
  - Sword above head: Cone in direction she's facing
  - Sword to the side: Large circle AoE centered on 2P
- Golden tethers: Teleport attacks. Watch for tether that pulses.
- Reproduce: Clones repeat attack of 2P
- Three Parts Disdain: Stack marker, keep away from walls (three knockbacks)
- Four Parts Resolve: Player 1,3 Circle AoE, Player 2,4 Line AoE

- Compound Pod: R011: Line AoE - Resolve in two steps:
  - Identify current safe zone: Only square without pod on North and West side
  - Watch for end location of first tp'd pod: If in your way, move to lane of other pod being tp'd
  - Watch end location of second tp, and adjust one once more for final safe spot

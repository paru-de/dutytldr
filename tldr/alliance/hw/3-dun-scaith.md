## 1/7 [Deathgaze Hollow]

### Healer

**Raidwide**:
**Doom**: Cast by Doomsay on up to 2 random players. Esuna.

### Notable mechanics
- Void Death: Continuously run towards walls.  
- Void Aero II: Drop at the edges.

## 3/7 [Ferdiad Hollow]

### Tank

**TB**: Jongleur’s X

### Healer

**Raidwide**: Flameflow (scales in damage with incorrectly colored fire/water spouts)

### Notable mechanics
- Wormhole
  - Big blue orb: Big Circle AoE around Atomos hit.
  - Golden Pretzel: Stand beneath Atomos (Donut).
- Debilitator: Everyone will either get Fire Resistance Down or Water Resistance Down debuff
  - Red (Fire) Debuff: Walk through fire spouts.
  - Water (Blue) Debuff: Walk through water sputs.
- When Ferdiad moves to center surrounded by Atomos, get out of their direct path. Kill all adds.
- Jester's Reward: Half-wide cleave immediately after adds.
- Wormhole will now perform two attacks at the same time. Blue out, Gold, in!

## 5/7 [Scathach]

### Tank

**Keep add away from boss**

**TB**: 

### Healer

**Raidwide**: Thirty Souls, Blinding shadow (after cocoon phase)

### Notable mechanics
- Don't stand with tank.
- When she jumps up, get out of melee range.
- Shadespin: Hourclock AoE. Watch for raised wing, AoE in its direction.
  - Sometimes followed up by a dash, get away from her.

- Cocoon phase: Kill adds, stand in circles (Towers). 
   - Chimera - Dragon's Voice: Get in (Donut). Ram's Voice: Get out.

## 7/7 [Diabolos]

### Tank
*Tower phase: Get into the center circle.*

**TB**: [L] Hollow Camisado. 
  - Very hard hitting due to him critting. Consider using invulnerability.

### Healer

**Raidwide**: Ruinous Omen, Ultimate (after Life Gate), Pavor Inanis, Hollow Omen

**Doom**: Hollow Nightmare (Esuna)

### Notable mechanics
- Ultimate Terror: Stand close to boss (Donut)
- Shadethrust: Line AoE in front of boss.
- Death & Lifegate: Kill gates. Lifegate has multiple lifes.
- Boss now has guaranteed crits.
- Stack marker with eye: Stack up, face away from marked player.
- Glowing gate: Some players must move into it and kill an add.
- Tower phase: Do not go into the center circle. Tanks only.
- Earth Shaker: Spread out. Conal AoE starting from boss position towards marked players.

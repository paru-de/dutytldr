## 2/7 [Arachne Eve]

### Tank

**TB**: Dark Spike

### Healer

**Raidwide**: Implosion

### Notable mechanics
- Burrow under the ground: Expanding AoE's traveling outwards. Move into first AoE after it goes off. Followed by proximity marker.
- Green AoE: Move away from raid. Tethered players can be freed by running through tethers.
- Webs above arena: Avoid green areas. Intercept orbs before they reach the boss. Kill spiders, jump through holes, kill pillars and add.
- Yellow marker: Move away from others. Anyone close by will be pulled up to web area.
- Red Marker: Drop at edge of arena. Creates web patches.
- Vortex: Stand in web patches or you get sucked in.

### Tank
*Tank her in center of arena*


### Healer

**Raidwide**: [Multi] Dancing Mad

### Notable mechanics
- Do not stand with tank (cleave attacks will hit them).
- Haircut: Cleaves left or right side of arena. Watch for hair with a scythe, indicating the side to be hit.
- Extension: Everyone caught in purple trap will be trapped. Attack cage to free them.
  - Flowers: Move close to the axe flowers. Avoid being in the line of scythes.
  - **Penetration**: If she moves to the middle of the room, look away and position yourself to be knocked back in-between flower seeds.

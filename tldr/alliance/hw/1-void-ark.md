## 2/7 [Cetus]

### Healer

**Raidwide**: Immersion

### Notable mechanics
- Adds: Players marked with green swirls must move away from raid.
- Body Slam (2nd cast): Now followed by Donut (stand close to boss) or Out (move away from boss).

## 3/7 Forward Port Ventilator

*Alliances split. Prioritize the Pagan's Knots. If not killed fast enough they will put up a poison cloud. Clear it with the lit up circle platforms (all three platforms need players in it).*

## 4/7 [Irminsul and Sawtooth]

### Tank
*Both bossed need to be tanked, separate them*

### Notable mechanics
*Bow buff: Boss is resistant to range attacks. Staff buff: Boss is resistant to magic attacks.*

- Mucus Bomb: Move away from raid. Anyone caught will be tethered. Move through tethers to free them.
- Mucus Spray: Stand close to Sawtooth (Donut). Same as Mucus Bomb.
- Insects, Black bubbling circles: Kill insects. Anything in circles will be eaten after a few seconds, filling Enrage meter.
  - Loss of control and small Conal attacks if you get eaten.
- **Shield around Irminsul, Shockwave Stomp**: Break Line of Sight with Sawtooth. Stand behind Irminsul.

## 6/7 [Cuchulainn]


### Healer
*Constantly stacking bleeding debuff. Can't be removed. Idol of Impurity adds inflict additional stacks.*

**Raidwide**: Pestilence. Large damage if any adds were eaten by boss.

### Notable mechanics
- Corrosive Bile: Conal AoE in direction of targeted player.
- Flailing Tentacles: Stand in-between tentacles.
- Idol of Impurity: Destroy Gyrtowers.
- Void Pact: Kill adds, prioritize the one targeted with a Conal AoE. If they reach the boss, he gets a damage up. 
- Pestilence: Stand on raised platform.
- Black Lung: Run through orbs.
